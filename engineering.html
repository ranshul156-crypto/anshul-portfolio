Nov 2025 — present · Live

Akshata Cycle Tracker

My contribution: lead developer for the forecasting engine and backend (Firebase Auth/Firestore, security rules, deployment) of a menstrual-health web app for Akshata Foundation — rebuilt from a team prototype that had never deployed. The forecast is deliberately low-variance: two input sequences only (cycle and period lengths), an exponentially-weighted mean under four cycles, and a Huber-robust autoregression blended with that mean from four cycles up. Verified against the deployed production bundle, Oct 2026.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Firebase
  • Robust regression

Oct 2026 — present · Bench prototype

Wearable sensing prototype

My contribution: self-directed hardware work with friends who lent boards — ESP/Arduino-based PPG sensing, an NTC thermistor front end, serial logging, and a phone-camera PPG pipeline. First versions short-circuited and read noise; a 3D-printed mount sealed with glue against sweat produced the first version that survived being worn. Built to test GutGlow's artefact hypothesis where it actually lives: at the sensor.

  • ESP / Arduino
  • KiCad
  • Signal conditioning
  • PPG
